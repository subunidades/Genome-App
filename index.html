The brain-slice and genome-map dot values are illustrative: they are hand-curated approximations of well-known patterns, not measurements from the Allen Human Brain Atlas, and the anatomy is a simplified composite lateral slice with deep structures projected onto it. Chromosome sizes (GRCh38) and gene positions are approximate. The cortical cell-type bars are real: Allen human cortex single-nucleus data (trimmed means per cell-type cluster, 120 clusters averaged into 17 classes). For regional data, download the atlas from human.brain-map.org.
