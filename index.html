About the data and its limits

Regional expression is real Allen Human Brain Atlas microarray data (six donors averaged, each gene scaled 0 to 1 across regions). The brain outlines are traced from the Allen Human Reference Atlas 3D v2 (MNI152 template, gyral annotation) as a composite lateral view of the left hemisphere: the cortical lobes show the real lateral surface and the deep structures are projected onto it. The hippocampus and white-matter shapes are schematic. Chromosome sizes (GRCh38) and gene positions are approximate. The cell-type bars are real: Allen whole-human-brain single-nucleus data (Siletti et al. 2023, about 3.4 million nuclei), averaged per cell type (31 Allen superclusters) within each region. Allen names its tissue samples by dissection, and these are mapped onto the 16 regions by name, so some assignments are approximate: for example the entorhinal areas count toward hippocampus, and cortical areas are assigned to the four lobes. White matter has no cell-type data because none of the Allen tissue samples used here are white matter. Cell-type data licence: CC BY-NC 4.0. Data: Allen Institute for Brain Science (api.brain-map.org, brain-map.org).
