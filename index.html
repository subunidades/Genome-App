About the data and its limits

Regional expression is real Allen Human Brain Atlas microarray data (six donors averaged, each gene scaled 0 to 1 across regions). The brain outlines are traced from the Allen Human Reference Atlas 3D v2 (MNI152 template, gyral annotation) as a composite lateral view of the left hemisphere: the cortical lobes show the real lateral surface and the deep structures are projected onto it. The hippocampus and white-matter shapes are schematic. Chromosome sizes (GRCh38) and gene positions are approximate. The cortical cell-type bars are real: Allen human cortex single-nucleus data (trimmed means per cell-type cluster, 120 clusters averaged into 17 classes). Data: Allen Institute for Brain Science (api.brain-map.org, brain-map.org).
